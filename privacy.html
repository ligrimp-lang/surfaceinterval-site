Privacy Policy

Surface Interval — underwater video colour and dive reels

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Surface Interval collects nothing. This document exists because Apple requires every app to have one (App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(i)), not because there is much to disclose.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code of any kind in this app.

Network access

The app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no networking code at all. Nothing on your phone can leave it through this app, because there is no mechanism by which it could.

One thing looks like network access and is not: a video you keep in iCloud rather than on the phone. When the app needs to read such a video, it asks Apple's Photos framework for it, and Photos fetches it from your own iCloud account over your own connection. That is a conversation between your phone and Apple, on your behalf, in the same way the Photos app itself works. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no "us" to send it to.

Your videos

Your videos stay where they are. The app reads them in place — in your photo library, or on a card or drive you pick through Files — and never copies them into itself. It never deletes anything you shot.

What it writes is what you ask it for: a reel saved to your photo library, with your permission, at the moment you tap save. Nothing else is written anywhere outside the app's own storage on your iPhone.

What is stored, and where

  • The list of videos you added, and the folders you put them in — names, dates, and what the app measured about the colour of each one.
  • The reels you have made, and their settings.
  • Working files while a reel is being made: frames, thumbnails and the pieces of the reel itself.
  • An export record — what the app did, how long it took, and what went wrong when something did.
  • Your settings.

All of it is written inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is included in your device backup, so restoring a new phone brings it back. If you back your phone up to iCloud, Apple's backup includes it — that is between you and Apple, and it is how your data follows you to a new device. Nothing is sent to us.

Deleting it

Settings › Clear the working files removes the frames, thumbnails and reel pieces. Deleting the app removes everything else with it. Neither leaves a copy anywhere, because no copy was ever made.

Music

The music that goes under a reel is bundled in the app, from Pixabay under its Content License. It plays from inside the app; no track is fetched, and no listening of any kind is recorded or reported.

Children

The app is a tool for editing your own dive video and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version appears here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new disclosure.
